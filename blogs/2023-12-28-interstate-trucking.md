You’re diving into interstate trucking, where understanding key regulatory requirements is vital for success. You’ll need a USDOT number , an MC number , and a valid commercial driver’s license (CDL) for your drivers. Drivers required to hold a CDL must undergo specific testing protocols, including knowledge and skills tests, especially if they operate vehicles over a certain weight or those designed to transport a significant number of passengers or hazardous materials.
